How genotypes are scored here

Each mutation adds a penalty score for each drug; some combinations add more. The total maps to a level, modelled on the Stanford HIVdb approach:

{% for lvl, label in levels.items() %}{% endfor %}
{{ lvl }}{{ label }} {{ ['score < 10','10–14','15–29','30–59','≥ 60'][lvl - 1] }}

A drug's contribution to the genotypic susceptibility score (GSS) is 1 (levels 1–2), 0.75 (3), 0.5 (4) or 0 (5). After virological failure, aim for at least two fully active drugs, or a fully active high-barrier anchor (dolutegravir or boosted darunavir).

The table is abridged and approximate, for teaching. Use the laboratory report or Stanford HIVdb clinically.
