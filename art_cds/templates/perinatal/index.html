BHIVA §11.1 low-risk criteria

All of: ART started at least 10 weeks before delivery; good engagement with services; at least one viral load in the 6 weeks before delivery; all viral loads < 50 copies/mL in the 10 weeks before delivery. A viral load of 50–200 copies/mL can be assessed individually, especially if a later one is < 50.

Low risk → 2 weeks of zidovudine. Otherwise combination PNP: zidovudine and lamivudine for 4 weeks with nevirapine for 2 weeks. Start within 4 hours of birth. Dose from Appendix 4 of the guideline.
